Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

reel-bots ("the app") is a private automation operated by its owner (contact below). It creates geography quiz videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube and Instagram channels. The app is used only by its owner.

Data the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API (scope youtube.upload) only to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, store or share any other data from Google accounts, and it does not access data of any other person.

Data from visitors and viewers

The app does not collect, store or process personal data of visitors, viewers or other users. This website uses no cookies and no tracking.

Storage and sharing

The access credentials needed for uploading are stored encrypted (GitHub Actions secrets) and are never shared with third parties. No data is sold or transferred.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
